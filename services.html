Role-based control

Different responsibilities.
Appropriate access.

Your application can include separate owner, manager and employee panels. Permissions are defined for your project, not simply different-looking screens.

Owner

A high-level view of the business, user management and settings relevant to the agreed scope.

Manager

Operational oversight, task coordination and team information appropriate to the manager's responsibilities.

Employee

Access to assigned tasks and the information needed for the employee's work, without unnecessary administrative access.

These are examples of a possible role structure. The final permission model is agreed for each project.

Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

Can I request a custom project now?

Yes. Zerone International LLC accepts project inquiries and prepares proposals. Email us with your requirements to start the discussion.

Can the application have multiple user panels?

Yes. Owner, manager and employee panels can be included where appropriate. Their permissions and features are defined in the project scope.

Do you guarantee integration with every marketplace?

No. Integrations depend on platform policies, available APIs, account access and technical feasibility. We review these requirements before promising an integration.

How much will my project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope and requirements. Contact us for a proposal; there is no universal price or delivery timeline.

Are your crosslisting and lot-analysis products already available?

Our crosslisting system and liquidation lot-analysis tool are in development. Custom project inquiries are a separate service and are welcome now.

What about source-code ownership and ongoing support?

Ownership, hosting, maintenance and support terms are specified in the proposal or agreement. Contact us to discuss what you need.
