## Starter — $149 a month

**Setup: $399** (half when you order, half when your site goes live)

**You get:**

- a professional, mobile-friendly website on your own domain;
- hosting, security and backups;
- up to 4 small edits a month (new hours, prices, photos);
- your Google Business Profile kept accurate: hours, categories, services and photos;
- a short monthly report in plain words.

**What it replaces:** a web designer, a separate hosting bill, and chasing someone every time your hours change.

**The math:** for a $35 haircut, about 5 extra customers a month cover it. For a $300 repair, one extra job covers two months.

## Growth — $249 a month

**Setup: $499** (half when you order, half when everything is live)

**You get everything in Starter, plus:**

- a review request after every job, sent to all of your customers (by e-mail; by text message once your carrier registration is approved);
- reply drafts for every new review, ready for you to approve;
- an online booking or quote form that reaches you instantly;
- missed-call text-back: callers you couldn't answer get a text with a link to book or ask. *Text-message features need a carrier registration for your business, which usually takes 2–4 weeks. Until then, the plan runs without them.*

**What it replaces:** remembering to ask every customer for a review, and calling back everyone who got your voicemail.

**Why it matters:**

- 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, and 74% look for reviews written in the last three months (BrightLocal, 2026).
- 80% are more likely to use a business that replies to all of its reviews (BrightLocal, 2026).
- In one 2024 test of 85 small businesses, only about 4 in 10 calls were answered by a person (411 Locals).
