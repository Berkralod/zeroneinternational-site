Most customers find a local business on their phone, often in the evening, and decide in a minute or two. **ZERONE International — Local Services** helps small businesses on Long Island make that minute count: accurate information, a page that answers their questions, and a fast reply when they get in touch.

We are a small company based in Farmingville, NY. Everything we do can be handled in writing, by e-mail or chat, so you never have to find time for a long phone call.

## What we can do for your business

- **A website that works on every phone.** Your services, prices, opening hours, a map, "Call" and "Directions" buttons, and a quote or booking form. It runs on your own domain. Hosting, security and small edits are included in the monthly plan.
- **An accurate Google Business Profile.** We fix your hours, categories, services and photos, and keep them up to date. The profile itself is free from Google. Our fee is only for our work.
- **A steady flow of reviews.** After each job, every customer gets a short, polite request with a link. We ask all of your customers, not only the happy ones, and we never offer rewards for reviews. Both are Google's rules.
- **Replies that don't fall through the cracks.** Reply drafts for every new review, ready for you to approve.
- **Missed-call text-back.** When you can't answer, the caller gets an instant text with a link to book or ask a question. *This needs a carrier registration for your business, which usually takes 2–4 weeks.*
- **An AI phone assistant** *(coming soon)*. It answers your calls around the clock, says that it is an AI assistant, takes messages, books appointments and texts you anything urgent. It is not an emergency service.
- **A short monthly report.** Website visits, new requests, reviews and calls, in plain words.

## How it works

1. **We look at what your customers see today.** Your listing, your website (if you have one) and how easy it is to reach you. This is free.
2. **We show you a free preview.** A private page with ideas for your business. It is not public and it is deleted after 30 days.
3. **You choose what you need.** We agree on everything in writing. Then we build it and show it to you before it goes live.
4. **We keep it up to date.** Every month we make your small edits, watch that everything works, and send your report.

## What stays yours

- Your domain, your Google Business Profile and your accounts are always in your business's name. We are added as a helper, and you can remove us at any time.
- We never ask for your passwords on paper, by text message or by e-mail.
- Monthly plans have a 3-month minimum, then run month to month. If you leave, we hand over your website files and help you move.
