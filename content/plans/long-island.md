Long Island is where we live and work. Zerone International LLC is based in Farmingville, in the middle of Suffolk County, and most of the businesses we talk to are a short drive away: contractors in Ronkonkoma, salons in Massapequa, auto shops in Hicksville, restaurants in Patchogue.

Long Island customers search close to home. Someone in Levittown looking for an electrician wants one who will come to Levittown, not a company forty miles away. That makes three things matter more than anything fancy:

- **An accurate Google Business Profile**, with the right service area, hours, categories and recent photos.
- **A fast, mobile-friendly website** that says clearly which towns you serve and has one-tap "Call" and "Request a quote" buttons.
- **Someone, or something, that answers the phone.** Call tracking companies report that a large share of business calls go unanswered. [CallRail puts it at 28%](https://www.callrail.com/blog/how-to-close-missed-call-customer-gap) of inbound calls on average across its customers. A caller who gets voicemail often tries the next name on the list.
