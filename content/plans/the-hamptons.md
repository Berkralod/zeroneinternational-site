Running a business in the Hamptons means running two businesses in one year. From late spring through the end of summer, the South Fork fills with visitors and second-home owners, and restaurants, shops, landscapers, pool services, house cleaners, caterers and boat businesses in the Towns of Southampton and East Hampton are at full speed. After the season, the pace drops, hours change, and many businesses close for part of the winter or work with a smaller team.

Customers here behave differently too. Many are visitors or part-time residents who do not know local businesses by name. They search on their phone from the beach, the car or a rental house, often late in the evening, and they expect an answer quickly. Others are homeowners who live elsewhere for most of the year and arrange house care, repairs and openings from a distance, by phone or text.

That mix creates a familiar problem: in summer there are more calls than people to answer them, and in the off-season the information online is often out of date.

## Questions from Hamptons business owners

### We are closed for part of the winter. Is a monthly plan worth it?

That depends on your business, and we will tell you honestly. Many seasonal businesses still get searches and calls in the off-season, for bookings, gift cards, catering or next year's work. Your profile and website should say clearly when you reopen and how to reach you.

### Can Ava handle reservation or booking questions?

Ava can answer common questions, take the caller's details and book appointments, and it texts you anything that needs your attention. It always says it is an AI assistant. It is not an emergency service.

### Our customers include people who live in the city. Does that matter?

It does, and it is a reason to make everything work by text and e-mail. A homeowner in Manhattan should be able to book a spring opening or ask a question without waiting for a callback.

### When should we start?

Any time works, but setting things up before the season starts gives you time to review everything without pressure.
