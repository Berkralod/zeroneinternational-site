For most local searches, the Google Business Profile shows up before any website. When someone in Smithtown searches for a "landscaper near me", the map results with names, stars, photos and a "Call" button are often all they look at. A profile with the wrong category, old hours or no photos loses those customers quietly.

## What we check and fix

- **Business type.** Storefront or service-area business. Contractors and mobile services should hide their address and set real service areas. Getting this wrong is a common reason profiles get suspended.
- **Name and categories.** Your real business name with no extra keywords, and the primary category that matches what you do most.
- **Services, hours and holiday hours**, kept accurate through the year, including the summer season out east.
- **Photos.** Recent, real photos of your work, team and vehicles, within Google's size rules.
- **Reviews.** A polite review request to every customer after each job (not only the happy ones, and never for a reward: those are Google's rules), and reply drafts for every new review that you approve.
- **Posts and updates** so the profile looks active.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes, the profile itself is free from Google. Our fee is only for our work.

### Can you get me to the top of Google Maps?

No one can promise rankings. We make sure your profile is complete, accurate and active, which is what Google says it looks at.

### Will you need my login?

We ask you to add us as a manager on your profile. The profile stays yours, and you can remove us any time.

### My profile is suspended. Can you help?

We can review what may have caused it and help you prepare a reinstatement request, but Google makes the final decision.
