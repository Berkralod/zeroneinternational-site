If you run a plumbing, electrical, roofing, HVAC or remodeling business on Long Island, most new customers find you the same way: they search on their phone, look at two or three companies, and call the one that looks real and answers. Your website and your Google Business Profile do that first job for you, usually while you are on a job site.

## What a contractor website needs (and what it doesn't)

- **The towns you actually serve.** A homeowner in Commack wants to know you come to Commack. We list your real service area in plain words, and match it to your Google profile.
- **One-tap "Call" and "Request a quote" buttons** that work with a thumb, on every page.
- **Real photos of your work.** Before-and-after shots beat stock photos every time.
- **Licenses and insurance.** If you have them, we show them clearly.
- **Fast loading on a phone.** Plenty of searches happen in a driveway or a basement.

You don't need a blog with fifty posts, a chat pop-up or animations. A clear, fast site with honest information does more.

## Calls you can't take on the roof

Contractors miss calls because they are working, not because they don't care. Our AI phone assistant, Ava, can answer when you can't: it says it is an AI, asks your intake questions (address, problem, how urgent) and texts you the urgent jobs right away. On the Growth plan, missed-call text-back sends the caller a link to book or ask, so the job doesn't go to the next name on the list.

## FAQ

### Do I need a storefront to show up on Google Maps?

No. Contractors can set up a Google Business Profile as a service-area business with the address hidden. See our [service-area guide](/blog/google-business-profile-service-area-business/).

### Will the website and domain be mine?

Yes. The domain, the Google profile and all accounts stay in your name. If you leave, we hand over the website files.

### Can you guarantee more jobs?

No, and nobody honestly can. We make it easier for customers to find you, trust you and reach you.

### How fast can it go live?

That depends on how quickly we get your photos and details. The written proposal you receive after the first call includes a timeline.
