Suffolk County is our home. Zerone International LLC is based in Farmingville, in the Town of Brookhaven, and we work with small businesses across the county, from Babylon and Huntington in the west to Riverhead and the East End.

Suffolk is a large county, and that shapes how local businesses work. A plumber, landscaper or electrician may drive from Smithtown to Patchogue to Mastic in a single day. Many owners spend most of their hours on job sites or in a truck, not at a desk. When a customer calls, the owner is often under a sink, on a roof or behind the wheel. Customers, meanwhile, search on their phones for someone "near me" and call the first business that looks trustworthy and answers. If no one picks up, many simply try the next name on the list.

Search results here are also very local. Someone in Islip searching for a dentist or an auto shop expects results close to home, not two towns over. An accurate Google Business Profile with the right service area, hours and photos matters as much as the website itself.

- **Seasonal businesses on the North and South Fork.** Farm stands, wineries, marinas and rental businesses on the East End often need different hours, menus and messages in summer and winter. We keep your website and profile updated as the season changes.

## Questions from Suffolk County business owners

### Do you really meet in person?

Yes. We are based in Farmingville, so meetings in Brookhaven, Islip, Smithtown, Babylon, Huntington or Riverhead are easy to arrange. Further out on the East End, we can meet in person or handle everything in writing, whichever suits you.

### I don't have a shop. Can I still show up on Google Maps?

Yes. Google lets businesses that visit customers, such as contractors and cleaners, hide their address and show a service area instead. We set that up correctly and list the towns you serve.

### Will Ava pretend to be a person?

No. Ava says at the start of the call that it is an AI assistant. It takes messages, answers common questions and books appointments. It is not an emergency service. You can hear it yourself by calling our own line.

### Do I need a new website?

Not always. Sometimes the existing site only needs fixes so it works on phones and loads quickly. We tell you honestly what we see in the free review, and you decide.
